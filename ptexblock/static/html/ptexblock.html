PTE Read Aloud is scored on three criteria: content, oral fluency, and pronunciation.

  • Content: You are rewarded for reading the text exactly as written. Omitting, adding, or replacing words lowers this score.
  • Oral fluency: Measures how smooth and natural your speech is. Hesitations, repetitions, and false starts reduce this score.
  • Pronunciation: Measures how clear and understandable your speech is to a native speaker. You don’t need a native accent, but your speech must be easy to understand.
